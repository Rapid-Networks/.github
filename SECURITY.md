# Security Policy

## Supported versions

We fix security issues in the latest release (or `main`, for repos without
releases). Older versions are not supported.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do **not** open a public issue, pull request or discussion for
security problems.

Report privately in either of these ways:

- Use **Report a vulnerability** under the repo's **Security** tab
  (GitHub private vulnerability reporting), or
- Email dev@rapidnetworks.co.za.

Please include:

- a description of the issue and its impact
- steps to reproduce or a proof of concept
- affected repo and version or commit, if known

## Scope

This policy covers the code in the Rapid Networks GitHub organisation.

Out of scope:

- denial-of-service or load testing
- social engineering or phishing of staff or customers
- physical attacks against our network or premises
- testing against our production network or customer services

## What to expect

- We'll acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
- We'll confirm whether the issue is valid and keep you updated as we work
  on a fix.
- We follow coordinated disclosure: please give us **90 days** from your
  report to release a fix before disclosing publicly. We'll agree on a date
  with you, and may ask for more time for complex issues.
- With your permission, we're happy to credit you when the fix is released.

## Safe harbour

We won't pursue legal action against good-faith research that follows this
policy. Please:

- avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption
- don't access, modify or keep customer data; if you come across any,
  stop and tell us
- only test against systems and accounts you own or have permission to use
